# Vsale_Store_Analyis
🛒 VSales Store Dataset — SQL Analytics Project

**Author: MC Vikas
**
A SQL-driven analysis of a retail store chain's daily sales transactions — covering order details, customer information, product categories, order timing, and order status — aimed at helping the business make data-driven decisions.

📌 **Project Background**

A retail store chain tracks daily sales transactions, including order details, customer info, product categories, order times, and order status. The business wants to optimize operations, improve customer experience, and increase profitability using data-driven decisions.

❗ **Problem Statement**

The store doesn't have a clear picture of:

Which products sell the most
Customer preferences
Which items bring in the most profit
Where things go wrong in delivery or operations

Because of this, the business is missing chances to earn more, losing customers, and making poor decisions.

**Solution**: Build proper reports and simple insights — using SQL — to understand sales, customers, and product performance better.

🎯 Why It Matters

Without proper insights, the business faces:

* Missed sales opportunities
* Poor inventory and staffing decisions
* Increased operational costs
* Low customer satisfaction
* Inaccurate business forecasts

Solving this helps increase revenue, improve service quality, and optimize operations.
